## Monthly model

Prepare base, downside, and upside cases with monthly rows for opening cash, revenue, payroll, contractors, infrastructure, legal/compliance, sales/marketing, insurance, taxes, other spend, net burn, ending cash, and runway.

## Hiring plan

For every proposed role record start month, loaded monthly cost, milestone unlocked, dependency, downside treatment, and approval state. Do not list “3–4 hires” unless the model funds each role.

## Milestone financing

Tie capital to acceptance evidence for durable deployment, source-to-runtime identity, security review, selected buyer discovery, design-partner conversion, paid pilot, and repeatable implementation.
