## Stage definitions

1. `DISCOVERY PLANNED` — target hypothesis only.
2. `INTERVIEW COMPLETED` — dated notes exist; not a partnership.
3. `PROBLEM CONFIRMED` — participant confirmed a defined problem in writing.
4. `PILOT SCOPING` — scope and success criteria under discussion; not committed.
5. `LOI EXECUTED` — signed non-binding or binding document retained and characterized exactly.
6. `PILOT CONTRACTED` — executed agreement and commercial terms retained.
7. `PAID` — reconciled payment received.
8. `PRODUCTION PROVEN` — contract, deployment, usage, reliability, and customer confirmation support the claim.

Record meetings and warm introductions accurately. Never transform them into customers, endorsements, pipeline value, or design-partner commitments.
