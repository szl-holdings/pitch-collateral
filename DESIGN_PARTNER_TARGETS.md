# Design-partner discovery plan

**Updated:** 2026-08-26  
**Status:** internal hypotheses; no organization or person is a customer, partner, endorser, or committed pipeline account without a dated primary record.

## Selection rule

Choose one segment before outreach. Score prospective interviews on:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Consequence | Does an AI-assisted action create material operational, financial, safety, or regulatory risk? |
| Evidence gap | Can the team prove what policy, evidence, artifact, and operator decision governed the action? |
| Urgency | Is there a funded workflow expected to deploy within twelve months? |
| Buyer | Is there an identifiable technical-risk owner and budget owner? |
| Integration | Can one bounded workflow be evaluated in weeks rather than quarters? |
| Expansion | Would a successful pilot extend to adjacent governed workflows? |

## Discovery funnel

1. Complete twenty structured interviews in one selected segment.
2. Obtain three to five written problem confirmations.
3. Define one pilot with baseline, success metrics, scope, data boundaries, security review, and exit criteria.
4. Convert one evaluated workflow into a paid pilot.

## Required interview record

- date, organization, role, and permission level;
- workflow and current alternative;
- consequence of failure;
- required evidence and approval boundary;
- security, data, deployment, and procurement constraints;
- implementation and buying timeline;
- decision owner, budget owner, and next step; and
- exact public wording, if any, the participant authorized.

## Evidence discipline

A conversation is not an endorsement. A demo is not a deployment. A warm introduction is not a partnership. A problem confirmation is not an LOI. An LOI is not revenue. No named relationship enters investor collateral without documentary authorization and permission to use it.
