## 1. Company

**Governed execution for consequential AI.** SZL is building a layer that makes policy admission, bounded execution, evidence receipts, and source-to-runtime identity inspectable.

## 2. Problem and buyer

AI systems are moving from answers to actions. Technical and risk leaders cannot approve consequential workflows when they cannot establish what policy admitted an action, what artifact ran, what evidence governed it, or what was refused.

- Revision-bound source, artifact, deployment, and runtime identity.
- Post-action verification.
