## Distribution rules

- Keep personally identifiable, financial, legal, customer, and security-sensitive documents in access-controlled storage; this repository contains indexes and status only.
- Do not place credentials, signed agreements, bank records, tax identifiers, customer data, or private correspondence in Markdown.
- Record an exact revision, observation date, verification method, and limitation for every promoted claim.
- Public source, CI, deployment, reachability, witnessed behavior, production use, customer adoption, and revenue are separate evidence states.
- Legal, tax, accounting, compliance, and investment conclusions require qualified professional review.

## Financing gate

External financing materials are ready for institutional diligence only when the operating model, cap table, entity/IP records, customer evidence, security posture, roadmap, and financing terms reconcile to the same dated assumptions. Until then, the company may present a bounded prototype and financing hypothesis, not completed Series A readiness.
