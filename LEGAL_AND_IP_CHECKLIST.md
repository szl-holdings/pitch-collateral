# Legal and intellectual-property diligence checklist

| Area | Required evidence | State | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entity | Formation, good standing, registered agent, jurisdictions | NOT YET PREPARED | Qualified counsel |
| Capitalization | Cap table, grants, options, SAFEs/notes, warrants, debt | NOT YET PREPARED | Counsel and finance |
| Founder IP | Executed invention and IP assignment | NOT YET PREPARED | Qualified counsel |
| Contributor IP | Contributor agreements and assignment/license schedule | NOT YET PREPARED | Qualified counsel |
| Open source | Dependency, license, notice, source-offer, and copyleft obligations | NOT YET PREPARED | Counsel/engineering |
| Trademarks | Names, marks, applications, conflicts, domains, social handles | NOT YET PREPARED | Trademark counsel |
| Patents | Inventions, disclosures, filings, public-disclosure dates | NOT YET PREPARED | Patent counsel |
| Data rights | Dataset sources, licenses, consent, restrictions, deletion duties | NOT YET PREPARED | Privacy counsel |
| Privacy | Privacy notice, retention, rights handling, subprocessors | NOT YET PREPARED | Privacy counsel |
| Commercial | Terms, DPA, MSA, SOW, NDA, warranty and liability posture | NOT YET PREPARED | Commercial counsel |
| Export/government | Export classification, sanctions, procurement representations | NOT YET PREPARED | Qualified counsel |

Do not upload privileged advice or executed sensitive agreements to ordinary repository history. This checklist records state and controlled-storage references only.
