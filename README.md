# SZL Holdings investor collateral

Private, evidence-bound materials for evaluating SZL Holdings. This repository is documentation, not a deployable product.

## Company position

SZL is building proof-carrying governed execution for high-consequence AI: policy admission, bounded execution, evidence receipts, and explicit refusal or uncertainty states. A11oy is the primary public prototype; Killinchu is a vertical demonstration of the same trust architecture.

The company is pre-revenue and founder-built. Public reachability is not production readiness, customer adoption, compliance authorization, or proof of a persistent deployment.

## Claim standard

Every promoted claim identifies its scope, revision or observation time, and primary evidence. These are separate states:

`designed → validated locally → pushed → CI-verified → artifact-attested → deployed → runtime-witnessed → production-proven`

Absence of evidence is `UNKNOWN` or `UNAVAILABLE`, never a pass. Formal claims name the bounded theorem and assumptions. Roadmap targets are not held certifications. Named relationships require documentary authorization and permission to use the name.

## Limitations

- No independently verified revenue, customers, paid pilots, production deployments, or compliance authorizations are claimed here.
- Unconditional Lambda uniqueness remains a conjecture. Bounded kernel results do not make the platform formally verified.
- Reachability does not establish durable persistence, artifact attestation, or production readiness.
- Signing, source-to-runtime parity, and deployment evidence vary by surface and must be checked at demonstration time.
